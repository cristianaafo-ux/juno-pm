Working brief / 01

Juno's draft

Evidence: limited sample

Problem: Requesters lack clear status and ownership after submitting a request. This may lead to duplicate follow-ups and manual status checks. The sample suggests this problem; it does not establish how often it occurs.

Desired outcome

01
For requesters Know whether a request was received and who owns the next step.
For the team Spend less time answering status-check questions.
